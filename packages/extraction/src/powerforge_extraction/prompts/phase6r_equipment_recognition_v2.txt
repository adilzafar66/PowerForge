readings, image, table cells or source metadata. Return only the structured result.
Copy version, stage and page_context_hash from the supplied envelope.

Inspect prose, nameplates, schedule rows and headers, notes, and diagram labels and symbols.
Use the page image alongside all pinned readings, including table readings. The layout, order,
and alternate readings are hints. Preserve conflicts and uncertainty. Use only the registry
equipment keys supplied, or null for an unknown type, and retain the original source description.
Give each observation a distinct local_ref. A tag may be PRESENT, ABSENT, UNREADABLE or AMBIGUOUS.
Keep distinct occurrences, including tag-less and repeated-tag observations, separately. Use the
source's installed, proposed, future, spare, reference, legend-only or unknown qualification.
An empty provision, reserved space, unused way, capacity count, or generic legend symbol
does not assert a physical device. Do not create equipment from those alone, even if a row
lists quantity, positions or a pole count. A genuine spare, future or proposed device needs
separate source support identifying equipment rather than only a space for it. Preserve the
qualification of such a device. Keep generic legend information as context when useful.

Check notes and upstream/downstream annotations for specifically identified equipment mentioned
by reference, such as a device identified by type and model. Preserve it as REFERENCE when
the source describes it as external or referenced. A reference does not establish installation
or topology. Do not infer connectivity, reconcile physical identities or merge across pages.

separate equipment, tag and context evidence. Otherwise use a short verbatim page-local quote
or an approximate normalized upright page box based on the visible image. Omit unsupported
citations. When the same model or tag occurs more than once, support each observation with
its own location, position identifier, or distinct reading occurrence. A shared quantity or
schedule row may support common context but cannot alone distinguish the devices. If the
page supports multiple observations but their individual positions are uncertain, preserve
that uncertainty and do not invent distinguishing evidence. Never invent readings, quotes or exact geometry. An empty list means no equipment
observations after reviewing the whole page. Do not silently omit observations to fit a limit.
