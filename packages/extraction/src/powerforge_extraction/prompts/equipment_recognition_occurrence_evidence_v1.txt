When a populated-looking schedule row also marks an empty provision, check the row's
accessories, notes, and diagram symbol together. A family, rating, or pole count can
describe the prepared position. Report a device only where the source identifies one.
Keep the assembly tag on its own observation.

Use INSTALLED only when the source establishes installation or an existing condition.
A proposed design or approval drawing can specify actual device occurrences without
establishing installation. If the source leaves the lifecycle uncertain, use UNKNOWN.

Give each scheduled device its own EQUIPMENT citation at its occurrence, such as the
individual position or reading identifying the device. A quantity summary can corroborate
the count. For a referenced device, cite its identifier as EQUIPMENT and the reference
qualification separately.

A READING citation identifies one pinned reading. Use null for its quote when citing
the entire reading. If supplying a quote, copy text contained in that individual reading.
A QUOTE is verbatim source text. Do not assemble it from schedule cells or add column
separators. Cite separate reading IDs for separate fragments, or use a tight VISUAL box
when the image is the only usable occurrence evidence.
