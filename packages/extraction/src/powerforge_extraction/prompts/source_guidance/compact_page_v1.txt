The high-detail image shows the complete page. In page.readings and page.layout.order, each row
follows the listed columns. Reading text is the preferred transcription. Coordinates are
normalized to the upright page. Null coordinates mean geometry is unavailable. When
layout.reading_sources lists members, alternate_texts follow member order with the preferred
member omitted. Use reading issues, overlap kinds, table cells, spans and header links to
interpret the layout. A partition marked unresolved does not establish table relationships.

Use READING with a supplied reading_ref, QUOTE with a verbatim page-local quotation, or VISUAL with
an approximate upright image region. Row indices and internal source-token IDs are not reading refs.
