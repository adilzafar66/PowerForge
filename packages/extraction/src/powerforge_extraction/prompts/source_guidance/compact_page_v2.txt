The high-detail image shows the complete page. Ordinary page.readings.rows and page.layout.order
rows follow their listed columns. A LINE or CELL group links readings in the pinned layout.
Its members follow member_columns. Half-open Unicode offsets [start, end) select each member's
literal text. Spaces between members are display separators. Each member retains its reading_ref
and upright normalized coordinates and inherits the group modality. Null coordinates mean
geometry is unavailable.

layout.support uses zero-based indices into [representation_ref, kind, source-token ID] rows.
Overlaps are [left index, right index, kind]. reading_sources rows contain [reading_ref, member
indices, preferred index]. Alternate texts follow member order with the preferred member omitted.
Cell boundary_support and unresolved support also refer to layout.support. Respect conflicts,
table cell and header links, and unresolved relationships. Grouping alone does not join measurements
or establish equipment ownership.

Use READING with an original reading_ref, QUOTE with a verbatim page-local quotation, or VISUAL
with an approximate upright image region. Groups and support indices are not reading refs.
