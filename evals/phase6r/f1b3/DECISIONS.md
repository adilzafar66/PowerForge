# F1-B2 recognition decisions

[Authoritative specification](../../../docs/specs/phase-6r-equipment-investigation.md#3-binding-f1-b2-architectural-decisions)

The revised objective is to recognize supplied equipment using the registry, retain identifiable equipment tags and supporting source locations, and ultimately publish unique equipment configurations within each document. Investigation A measures page-local recognition before imposing final uniqueness or requiring localization.

- **D01–D03 · Output and supplied scope.** Distinct identical physical devices can contribute to one final configuration. AI interprets which equipment belongs to the supplied scope. Empty spaces, alternatives, generic legends, reference-only equipment, out-of-scope equipment, and unclassifiable equipment do not become entries. Excluded content can still inform extracted attributes. The upstream Eaton breaker is reference-only.
- **D04–D06 · Recognition contract.** The target requires supported registry type, identifiable tag if present, and supporting source locations. Remove lifecycle qualification and evaluate the removal of source description and tag state. Consider server-owned local identifiers. The A1–A3 diagnostic contracts omit localization.
- **D07–D09 · Classification and evidence.** Explicit descriptions and justified engineering inference can establish a type. Omit unsupported or insufficiently classifiable equipment in the target workflow. Prefer specific type and tag sources, with approximate or justified whole-page evidence permitted. Stage 2 evidence requirements remain independent.
- **D10–D12 · Equivalence.** Compare type, tag, and complete established registry-associated attribute sets and values, including established inference and exact normalized equivalence. Mapping status, wording, raw unmapped data, citation counts and internal identifiers do not define identity. Partial, uncertain, invalid or unresolved values cannot prove equivalence. Differing tags or registry attributes keep entries separate. Joining tagged and untagged observations requires source-established identity.
- **D13–D14 · Scope and provenance.** Keep page-scoped model execution. Deduplicate only inside the same document. Preserve each supporting occurrence and location, including enough information to split a proposed group if Stage 2 reveals a registry-associated difference.
