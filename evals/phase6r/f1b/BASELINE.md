# F1-B Stage 1 baseline and correction review

The approved baseline used both table-enabled and positioned-only preparation, with two attempts per arm for seven development pages plus the ruled synthetic control. The Stage 1 prompt, provider schema, model, timeout, output cap and exact rendered image were held fixed within each page comparison. The benchmark used the production OpenAI adapter, server-owned recognition admission and candidate/evidence projection, with audited outputs in a private filesystem ledger. This isolated benchmark did not execute transactional publication to the product database or exercise its scheduler and lease path.

## Baseline outcomes

**32 requests were sent, 29 returned HTTP 200 and passed Stage 1 admission, and three had an unknown connection outcome.** Those three were Eaton with tables, Schneider positioned-only, and Siemens with tables. The adapter classified all three as transport connection errors, with no response headers or usage. They occurred on Docker transport and consumed their slots. The remaining unique slots were executed using the host transport with byte-identical, verified Linux-rendered images. No slot was retried. The 29 admitted response byte digests were rechecked against the private retention receipts. Because transport changed mid-run, the latency and admission differences should not be attributed to table layout.

Development metrics count failed pages in recall. Precision uses location-supported matches against emitted observations, with documented ambiguous source claims adjudicated separately.

- **Existing tables** · 12/14 development attempts admitted. The scorer matched 23 of 44 expected occurrences and 23 of 38 emitted observations. Strict recall was **52.3%**, precision **60.5%**, and F1 **56.1%**. All 23 matched types and tags were correct. Seven matched qualifications agreed with the reviewed fixture.
- **Positioned only** · 13/14 development attempts admitted. The scorer matched 30 of 44 expected occurrences and 30 of 37 emitted observations. Strict recall was **68.2%**, precision **81.1%**, and F1 **74.1%**. All 30 matched types and tags were correct. Eight matched qualifications agreed.
- **Matched development attempts** · The 11 pairs with both sides admitted had 26 expected observations per arm. Existing tables matched 15 and positioned only matched 22. The difference was dominated by citation behavior on a few drawings. Most development pages had no reconstructed table to remove. The available repeats do not identify a reliable causal table effect.
- **Ruled structural control** · All four replies identified the correct transformer type and TX-1 tag. All four used the shared schedule heading for EQUIPMENT evidence and the actual row identifier only for TAG evidence. The strict position scorer credited zero of four. The valid table and its header links did not resolve this model evidence-selection error.

The estimated standard token cost for the 29 attempts with reported usage is **US$0.080963**, using input, cached input and output list rates. Cache-write billing and the three unknown outcomes are excluded from that estimate. Reserved budget for the 32 sends was **US$17.06453760**. Median reported provider latency on admitted development pages was 5.48 s with tables and 5.05 s positioned-only. [GPT-6 Luna rates](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna)

## Failure traces and correction

- **Prepared spaces became physical devices on Schneider.** All three admitted replies emitted observations for positions 7 and 10. In one reply both were explicitly INSTALLED and each visual box localized to the corresponding provision location. The remaining two replies supplied incompatible or insufficient location evidence for those claims. The original schedule showed the positions' ratings and prepared-space markings. The proposals were already present in each original response and were admitted by the structural contract.
- **Source-recognized devices sometimes lost their evidence at localization.** One Schneider reply emitted the expected numbered breakers but used invented pipe-separated schedule rows as quotations. The localizer could not find those exact strings. Eaton's upstream model was correctly named and qualified in one reply, but its EQUIPMENT citation attached a multi-word quote to a single reading. Its exact model appeared only under a different evidence role, and the strict scorer therefore treated the occurrence as unverified. The model also omitted the upstream reference in another admitted Eaton reply. Those are distinct recognition and evidence failures.
- **Qualification drift was material.** The model sometimes labeled populated positions and an order BOM INSTALLED without affirmative lifecycle support. The approved fixture separately treats the Simson manufacturer datasheet as a REFERENCE. All eight Simson replies returned UNKNOWN for that model; these calls establish a qualification-policy discrepancy, not an installation claim. Field accuracy uses the approved gold policy and only the geometrically matched occurrences.
- **Coverage diagnostics remain separate.** None of the admitted replies hit the 128-observation limit. The review found no need to change the provider schema, deterministic equipment interpretation, Stage 2, or normalization. Strict position failures include real evidence defects and broad or unlocalized visual support. They are not all equipment recognition omissions.

The opt-in **`occurrence-evidence-v1`** prompt adds a concise schedule check for provision rows that also print a family and rating, requires affirmative source support for INSTALLED, and directs EQUIPMENT citations to the individual device or position. It clarifies that a READING quote must fit the referenced reading and that a QUOTE must be verbatim. The exact supplement is linked below and pinned by SHA-256 **`6010848e77d12838bd899075aa50c8a40c70ffc6b082dace5bef8c1dd23bd`**.

The default config remains `EXTRACTION_RECOGNITION_PROMPT_VARIANT=legacy`. Selecting the candidate pins a separate Stage 1 prompt identity. Stage 1 schema, Stage 2 prompt and schema, recognition admission, evidence localization and publication logic retain their existing contracts. Offline tests verified identical source projections across the prompt variants and a distinct candidate profile identity.

## Remaining approval and verification

The remaining approved-budget capacity is **30 calls**. Under separate authorization of the exact supplement, the proposal is 14 positioned-only development calls and 16 synthetic holdout calls, with zero retries and the cumulative **US$34** hard cap. Final selection requires the frozen candidate comparison, synthetic holdout, independent engineering holdout sources, and a later approved Stage 2 ablation before any permanent removal of table reconstruction.
